# Rotation-pet ASCII artwork

Feature 17 uses the user-selected, existing artwork below. The drawings are not
original Codotchi artwork and are **not relicensed under the project's MIT
licence**. Artist signatures are retained in terminal output. Only surrounding
whitespace is normalised for layout; silhouettes are not redrawn for moods or
life stages. Eggs, Classic and the people characters keep their previous art.

## Permission status — release blocker

Sources and signatures checked on 2026-11-23. The archive pages establish
provenance, **not an explicit licence to redistribute artwork in software**.
Permission for software redistribution has not been established for these six
pieces. Do not publish packages containing them until permission is recorded or
the user approves replacements with suitable documented licences. Approval of a
visual selection does not establish copyright permission.

The [Respect ASCII Artists Campaign](https://asciiartist.com/respectartistscampaign.html)
specifically warns against assuming all online ASCII art is freely reusable.
Retain this notice in packages and preserve signatures in any permitted reuse.
